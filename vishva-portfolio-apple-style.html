Meet Vishva.

Mechanical engineer with a B.E. from Coimbatore Institute of Technology, CGPA 8.53. R&D Engineer at HL Mando in Kanchipuram since June 2023.

My days are CATIA models, FEA review, root cause analysis, cost teardowns, and supplier feasibility. My goal is to turn more of those ideas into patentable improvements for automotive parts.
